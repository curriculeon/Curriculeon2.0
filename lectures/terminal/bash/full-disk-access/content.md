## Give Terminal Full Disk Access

* Open the `Spotlight Search` by holding `Command` and press `Space`.
* Open `System Preferences` by searching `System Preferences` in the `Spotlight Search` window.
* Click on `Security & Privacy`.
* Click on the `Privacy` tab at the top of the window.
* Scroll down and click on `Full Disk Access` in the left-hand menu.
* Click the lock icon in the lower-left corner of the window and enter your password.
* Click the `+` button.
* Select `Applications`
* Scroll to find and select `Utilities`
* Scroll to find and select `Terminal`

### Optional
* Restart your machine for safe measure.
